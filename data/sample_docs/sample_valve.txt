SAMSON Controls — Type 3531 Globe Control Valve
Technical Data Sheet | Revision 4.2

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Type 3531 is a pneumatically actuated single-seated globe control valve
for industrial process control applications.

VALVE BODY
Type: Globe valve (single-seated)
Nominal size: DN 25 to DN 150
Body rating: PN 16 to PN 40
Connection type: Flanged (EN 1092-1 / ASME B16.5)
Body material: 1.0619 (WCB) or 1.4408 (CF8M stainless steel)

PERFORMANCE DATA
Pressure rating: 40 bar (PN 40)
Differential pressure max: 32 bar (at shutoff)
Operating temperature: -10°C to +220°C (standard packing)
  Extended range: -60°C to +450°C (special packing available)
Leakage class: EN 60534-4 Class IV (standard) / Class VI (soft seat)

FLOW COEFFICIENTS (Kv values)
DN 25: Kv = 6.3
DN 40: Kv = 16
DN 50: Kv = 25
DN 80: Kv = 63
DN 100: Kv = 100
DN 150: Kv = 250

ACTUATOR (Type 3271)
Actuation: Pneumatic spring-return diaphragm
Air supply: 1.4 to 2.8 bar (fail-safe closed) or 3.0 to 6.0 bar
Positioner: SAMSON Type 3730 HART or EDP (optional)

TRIM MATERIALS
Plug: 1.4006 (410 stainless) or 2.4610 (Hastelloy C-276)
Seat: 1.4006 hardened / Stellite-faced
Packing: PTFE V-ring (standard) / Graphite (high-temperature)

WEIGHT (approximate)
DN 50 / PN 40: 18 kg (valve body only, without actuator)

CERTIFICATIONS
CE marking (PED 2014/68/EU)
SIL 2 capable (IEC 62061 / EN ISO 13849)
ATEX Zone 1 & 2 (with appropriate actuator)

STANDARDS
IEC 60534-1 (Industrial Process Control Valves)
EN 12266-1 (Pressure testing)
DIN 3840 (Face-to-face dimensions)
